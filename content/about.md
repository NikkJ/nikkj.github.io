Rübezahl is where I publish content about threat intelligence, malware analysis and breakdowns of public incidents.

## What's here

- **Malware analysis** — static and dynamic, with detection logic where it earns its place
- **Public incident breakdowns** — what happened, what was defensible, what wasn't
- Occasional arguments about how intelligence gets produced and consumed

## What's not here

No samples hosted on this site — hashes and MalwareBazaar links instead. Nothing
sourced from client environments. Nothing derived from private or underground
access. IOCs are defanged. Everything published here is TLP:CLEAR and comes from
public reporting or from samples I obtained publicly.

## Notes

I write in English; Portuguese is my first language. Corrections on either the
technical content or the prose are welcome.
